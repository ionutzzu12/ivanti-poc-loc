If you are a \{\{FRS\_VARS.Mobile\_Analyst-mhll-alhatf-almhmwl}} or a \{\{FRS\_VARS.Self\_Service\_Mobile\_portal-alkhdmh-althatyh-abr-alhatf-almhmwl}} user using the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Company}} \{\{FRS\_VARS.ProductName-Neurons-l-ITSM}} mobile app, you can get notifications on your mobile devices. This ensures you don't miss out on important updates, such as when a high priority incident or task is created or updated, or when a VIP incident is created or updated.

Notifications pop-up on your mobile device, clicking on the notification takes you to the **Notifications** corner on the \{\{FRS\_VARS.ProductName-Neurons-l-ITSM}} mobile app.

## Using the Notifications Corner

1. Log in to the \{\{FRS\_VARS.ProductName-Neurons-l-ITSM}} mobile app.
2. Click the **Notification** icon. If the **Notification** icon has a red mark, you have unread messages.
   - **Personal** tab - Has notifications directly sent you. All unread notifications in the **Personal** tab are in bold text.
   - **General** tab - Has notifications sent to all the employees. All notifications (read and unread) are all in normal text.
3. Click on a notification to view its details.

<Paragraph indent="1">
  Click **Clear All** to delete all notifications in the **Personal** tab. This is not available in the **General** tab.
</Paragraph>
