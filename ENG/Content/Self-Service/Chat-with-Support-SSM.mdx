You can either use the in-build chat feature of \{\{FRS\_VARS.ProductName}} or use Microsoft Teams to instantly message the Support/Service Desk Analyst team to help solve your issues or answer your questions in a one-on-one chat. You can also create incidents from the chat.

<hint type="info">
  If this feature is not available for you, your administrator might not have enabled it for you. Reach out to your organisation's administrator to get it enabled.
</hint>

## Using the Neurons for ITSM's built-in Chat

1. Tap the **Chat with support** icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/saj0ZP0UD7BxTMUWYBdGR_chatwithsupporticon.png" alt caption}.
   - A new chat window is displayed with the message "*We are online*" when the Support/\{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Service\_Desk\_Analyst-Service-Desk-Analyst}} staff is available. If not, the message "*Chat is unavailable at this time!*" is displayed.
   - Once a \{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Service\_Desk\_Analyst-Service-Desk-Analyst}} is assigned to your chat session, you will see the message "*Connected to \<*\{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Service\_Desk\_Analyst-Service-Desk-Analyst}}*&#x20;name>*" in the chat window.
2. Type your query or the issue you are facing in the text box and tap the send icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/TwC6WzDQJxTQzvtnPT6Iq_chatsendicon.png" alt caption}.

<Paragraph indent="1">
  The \{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Service\_Desk\_Analyst-Service-Desk-Analyst}} will reply to your query or issue. In case it cannot be solved through the chat, an Incident will be raised and the Incident number will be displayed on your chat window.
</Paragraph>

<hint type="info">
  If you stay idle on the chat for a long time, you will be logged-off after two warnings.
</hint>

## Using Microsoft Teams Bot

***Prerequisites:***

- You should have Microsoft Teams installed on your computer and have a client account created.
- You should have added the Teams app built for your organisation to your Teams client account.

<Paragraph indent="1">
  a. Log in to your Teams client account.
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph indent="1">
  b. Click **Apps** > select the Teams app your organisation has built > **Add to a chat**.
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph indent="2">
  Your organisation's Teams app is now added to your chat contacts which you can use to chat with Service Desk Analysts.
</Paragraph>

1. Log in to your Microsoft Teams client account.
2. Click the Teams app of your organisation that you added to your chat in the :Link[Prerequisites:]{label="Prerequisites:" overridedLabel="Prerequisites:" spaceId docId="O8vUIahWecZy2q95aHiOi" version="v2" docAnchorId="#Prerequisite" loadingMethod="dynamic" newTab="false" href="Chat-with-Support-SSM.mdx"} section.
3. To initiate a conversation with the bot, send a Hi.

<Paragraph indent="1">
  A pop-up with commands is displayed.
</Paragraph>

4. Select any of the following command:
   - **Create a Ticket** - prompts to enter the subject and symptom of your issue to create an incident. The bot creates an incident and displays the incident ID. The incident is then routed to the Support/Service Desk team to address it.
   - **Check the status of my ticket** - displays the list of all incidents and service requests created for/by you. Click one to view it's status.
   - **Chat with Support** - opens a chat session with an available Support/\{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Service\_Desk\_Analyst-Service-Desk-Analyst}} who will help resolve your issue.
   - **Leave Chat Conversation** - ends the chat session.
   - **Help** - displays a short description about each of the above commands.

<hint type="info">
  - If you stay idle on the chat, your session will expire after 3 warning messages based on the session expiry timeout setup by your administrator.
  - The options available to you may vary depending on how your organisation's administrator has set it up.
</hint>
