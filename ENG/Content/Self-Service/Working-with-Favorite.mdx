Using the Favourite option you can easily access your most frequently accessed Knowledge Articles, Service Catalogues, and Service Requests by adding them to Favourite.

<hint type="info">
  If the Favourite option is not available for you, then it has not been enabled by your \{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Administrator-Administrator.}}. Reach out to your \{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Administrator-Administrator.}} to get it enabled.
</hint>

## Adding and Removing a Favourite

### To add a record to Favorites

1. Log in to \{\{FRS\_VARS.Self\_Service\_Mobile\_portal}} portal.
2. Open the Knowledge/Service Catalogue/Service Request workspace.
3. Open a record or from the list view itself click the **Add to Favourite** icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/VzCRQe-pGM6hTmUoW7G9M_favorites-disabled.png" alt caption} for any of the record that you wish to add to Favourite.

### To remove a record from Favorites

4. Open the Knowledge/Service Catalogue/Service Request workspace.
5. Open a record or from the list view itself click the **Remove from Favourite** icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/Br_n5BJ2V_zX8CMLmLcHV_favorites-selected.png" alt caption} for any of record that you wish to remove from Favorites.

<hint type="info">
  - The Favourite option is also available in the **Search** workspace. You can add or remove records as Favourite from the **Search** workspace as well.
  - When you click the **Add to Favourite** icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/VzCRQe-pGM6hTmUoW7G9M_favorites-disabled.png" alt caption}, the icon toggles to **Remove from Favourite** icon :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/Br_n5BJ2V_zX8CMLmLcHV_favorites-selected.png" alt caption}.
</hint>
