<hint type="info">
  The \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} module is not available OOTB. This workspace appears only if your administrator has configured it for your role.
</hint>

The \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace allows you to access all articles available in the Knowledge Centre to find solutions or workarounds for your issues. It displays the most helpful and latest articles to assist you in troubleshooting your queries.

<Image src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/_cvv70o8uRduXSb4y4Zq1_knowledge-workspace-v3.png" position="flex-start" size="40" initialPath="../i/knowledge-workspace-v3.png" githubPath="self-ENG/Content/Self-Service/i/knowledge-workspace-v3.png"></Image>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Viewing Knowledge articles

  1. Log in to Neurons for ITSM.
  2. From the left menu, select \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}}.

  The list of \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} articles are displayed.

  3. Click an article to view the content.

  To search for a specific knowledge article, enter a keyword in the **Search** field (upper-right corner of the page) and press the **Enter**. The matching results are shown.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Search for a Knowledge articles

  1. In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace, enter a keyword in the **Search** field.
  2. Press **Enter**.

  The list of \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} articles are displayed.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Filter and Sort articles

  In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace, you can use **Filter & Sort** to narrow or organise the article list.

  To filter articles

  1. Click **Filter & Sort** dropdown list, and expand **Filter By**
  2. Choose from the following filter options:
     - **Author** - Mine
     - **&#x20;Assignment** - Unassigned, My Team, Mine
     - **Status** - Pending, Published, Pending Approval, In Review, Archived, etc.
     - **Published Date** - Today, This Week, This Month, This Year
     - **Effective Date** - Today, This Week, This Month, This Year
     - **More Option** - Linked to an - \{\{FRS\_VARS.Incident}}, or Problem, or CI
     - **Category** - Accessibility, Backup, Desktop - Hardware, Software, Phone, etc.
     - **User Rating** - Based on number of stars
     - **Created On** - From the last 7 days up to 1 year
     - \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} **type** - Document, Error Message, FAQ, Patch, Q\&A, Reference

  To sort articles

  1. Expand **Sort By**
  2. Choose a sort order: **Ascending** or **Descending**
  3. Select a sort field:
     - **Title**
     - **Description**
     - **Published Date**
     - **Keywords**
  4. Click **Apply** to update the articles.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Use Favorites to Save and View Knowledge Articles

  <hint type="info">
    This feature is available only if your administrator has enabled it for your role.
  </hint>

  You can mark important knowledge articles as favorites to easily access them later from a filtered view.

  **Mark an Article as Favourite**

  In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace, click the **Favourite** :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/w3jHw1pqbFKlogmXHV-f0_favorites-disabled15x12.png" alt caption} icon next to the article you want to save.

  - The icon changes to :inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/yzXkkCTORWlcwRG_AVHKZ/Br_n5BJ2V_zX8CMLmLcHV_favorites-selected.png" alt caption} show the article is now a favourite.
  - A confirmation message appears, and the article moves to the top of the list with your other favorites.

  **View your Favourite Article**

  In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace, under **Favourite Searches** select **My Favourite Knowledges** from dropdown list.

  - Your saved articles appear based on how your administrator has configured favourite views, such as **Urgent Articles**, **HR Policies**, or **All Published Knowledge Articles** .
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Provide feedback for an article

  1. In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} workspace, select the article you want to rate.

  <Paragraph indent="1">
    The selected \{\{FRS\_VARS.Knowledge-Knowledge}} article is displayed.
  </Paragraph>

  2. Click **Yes** or **No** under **Was this article helpful**?
  3. Under **Rate this article**, select a star rating:
     - 1 star = Not useful
     - 5 stars = Exactly what I was looking for
  4. (Optional) Enter detailed feedback in the **Feedback** section.
  5. Click **Submit Feedback**.
  6. To update your feedback later, click the icon, make your changes and then click **Update feedback**.
</ExpandableHeading>
