<hint type="info">
  The \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} module is not available OOTB. This workspace appears only if your administrator has configured it for your role.
</hint>

The \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} workspace allows you to view the latest messages and alerts, such as downtime notices, maintenance schedules, upgrade notifications, virus threats, and other bulletin-type communications shared across your organization.

You can view announcements. Only \{\{FRS\_VARS.Neurons\_ITSM\_-\_Administrator-Administrator.}} can create or edit them.

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Viewing Announcements

  1. Log in to \{\{FRS\_VARS.ProductName}}.
  2. From the left menu, select \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}}.

  The list of \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} are displayed.

  3. Click an \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} to view the following information:
     - Description
     - Urgency
     - Region
     - Created By
     - Effective Date
     - Expiration Date
     - Created On
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Search for an Announcements

  1. In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} workspace, go to the **Search** text field, enter a keyword related to the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}}.
  2. Press **Enter**.

  The workspace displays \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} matching your keyword.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Filter and Sort Announcements

  1. In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} workspace, click **Filter & Sort** from the dropdown list.
  2. Choose one of the following sort orders:
     - **Ascending**
     - **Descending**
  3. Choose a field to sort by:
     - **Subject**
     - **Urgency**
     - **Created Date Time**
     - **Description**
     - **Region**
  4. Click **Apply** to sort the list.
  5. To remove sorting, click **Reset All**.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Use Favourite Search filter

  <hint type="info">
    This feature is available only if your administrator has enabled it.
  </hint>

  1. In the \{\{FRS\_VARS.Announcements-Announcements}} workspace, click **All** from the dropdown list.
  2. Choose a specific announcement, such as **Urgent Announcements**, **HR Announcements**, or **Published Announcements**, to filter the list.

  The workspace display based on your selection.
</ExpandableHeading>
