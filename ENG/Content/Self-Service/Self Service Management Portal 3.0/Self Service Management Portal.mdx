The Self Service Management Portal V3 provides a simple interface and enables users to track requests for service. **Self Service&#x20;**&#x72;ole users can also access FAQs, announcements, knowledge articles, and chat with the Support team.

<hint type="info">
  Only users with Administrator role can add and configure a workspace to a role.
</hint>

The Self Service Management Portal V3 provides the following OOTB workspaces:

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" indent="2">
  [Service Catalogue](<Service Catalog.mdx>)
</Paragraph>

<Paragraph listStyleType="disc" indent="2">
  [My Items](<My Items.mdx>)
</Paragraph>

Only users with a&#x6E;**&#x20;Administrator** role can add Knowledge and Announcements workspaces and enable Alerts and FAQs for them to appear as workspaces on your Home page.
